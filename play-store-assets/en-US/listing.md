# Filmroll Play Store Listing Copy

App name: Filmroll: Vintage Camera

Short description (73/80):

Shoot and edit photos with live analog film looks and full-quality export

Long description:

Filmroll turns your phone into a focused analog-style camera and photo editor. Shoot through film looks in the live viewfinder, import photos you already have, and shape each frame with classic 3D LUT color, grain, warmth, contrast, and exposure controls.

Use Filmroll when you want digital photos to feel slower, warmer, and more intentional without giving up modern quality.

What you can do:

- Preview analog film looks live before you take the shot.
- Import existing photos and apply film 3D LUTs in one tap.
- Fine-tune strength, exposure, contrast, warmth, saturation, shadows, highlights, grain, and fringing.
- Hold to compare the original image against your edited frame.
- Save finished photos to your gallery in full resolution.
- Keep original metadata when exporting in the source format.
- Browse, search, and favorite film stocks for a faster editing flow.
- Download film LUTs for offline editing.
- Choose camera or gallery as your preferred starting point.

Filmroll is built for photographers, creators, and anyone who likes the mood of analog film but wants a clean mobile workflow. Frame the shot, pick a film stock, adjust the feel, and keep the final image in full quality.
